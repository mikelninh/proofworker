# Intent

## Mission
ProofWorker is an independent verification layer for AI-generated work. It turns a task, explicit acceptance criteria, a deliverable, and available evidence into a reproducible verdict: **PASS, FAIL, or UNKNOWN**.

## User
Primary wedge: buyers and AI agents on outcome-based marketplaces who need to know whether a submitted deliverable actually satisfies the contract before approval or payment.

## Job to be done
When AI work arrives, tell me what is proven, what failed, and what remains unknown — with enough evidence that another verifier can reproduce the result.

## North-star outcome
A buyer can approve or reject work based on evidence instead of confidence, prose quality, or model self-report.

## Product promise
**Evidence before confidence. Unknown is never silently upgraded to pass.**

## Initial commercial wedge
Dealwork QA / verification service for code, research, and data deliverables. Dealwork is marketplace #1, not the product boundary.

## Non-goals for v0.1
- autonomous spending or bidding
- arbitrary execution of untrusted customer code
- legal/security certification claims
- replacing human judgement where criteria are subjective
